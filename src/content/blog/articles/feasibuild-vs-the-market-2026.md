**Direct answer:** The 2026 feasibility-software market splits along two axes: traditional versus AI-powered, and enterprise pricing versus professional pricing. ARGUS and EstateMaster anchor the traditional-enterprise corner ($500–$3,000/mo); AIRE Software prices like a consultant (~$7K per study); an AI wave (Feasibility.pro, Buildora IQ, Feasly, Archistar) occupies subscriptions from ~$49 to ~$99/mo. FeasiBuild sits in the AI column on a different economic model — **$99 lifetime access plus per-report credits, no subscription** — and its payment-structure engine covers any market on earth: native presets for Dubai Law No. 8, Malaysia HDA and Australia's 10/90, plus configurable rules (no escrow, custom progress splits like 20/80 or 30/70, staged escrow with custom retention) selected by pin-drop in any country.

## 2. Head-to-head, named

**FeasiBuild vs ARGUS / EstateMaster.** The enterprise incumbents: deep configuration, decades of track record, quote-based licensing, real implementation projects and training curves. Payment-structure rules — escrow releases, progress percentages, retentions — are manual configuration, not engine logic; AI market research is not in the box. **They win:** large teams with IT and procurement. **FeasiBuild wins:** any professional who needs committee-grade output this week, approved on a credit card.

**FeasiBuild vs AIRE Software.** At roughly $7,000 per study, that's consultant economics with software branding. FeasiBuild's lifetime access costs about 1.4% of one such study — before credits. **They win:** mandated, fully managed engagements. **FeasiBuild wins:** everyone with a pipeline and a pulse.

**FeasiBuild vs Feasibility.pro ($99/mo).** The closest mirror price — monthly. Twelve months of their Pro equals twelve FeasiBuild lifetime accesses. Beyond pricing: check payment-structure configurability, export/ownership and where your data lives before choosing either. **They win:** teams preferring opex billing. **FeasiBuild wins:** ownership, lifetime economics, zero-knowledge design.

**FeasiBuild vs Buildora IQ ($69/mo) & Feasly ($49/mo).** Capable entry-level AI proforma tools for standard assets. The gaps that matter at committee level: a configurable payment-structure engine (escrow presets, custom splits, staged retentions), correlated downside shocks, and a bankable 30+ slide document rather than a dashboard. **They win:** light, frequent screening. **FeasiBuild wins:** anything that must survive a credit committee.

**FeasiBuild vs Archistar (from ~$63/mo).** Strong Australian generative site-planning and planning-feasibility angle. FeasiBuild goes deeper on the development-finance engine and works in any market via pin-drop — our own sample library spans a Malaysia warehouse, a UAE hotel and a China residential high-rise. **They win:** AU site-feasibility visuals. **FeasiBuild wins:** cross-border development finance.
